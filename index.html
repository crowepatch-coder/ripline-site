Ripline

A cut list optimizer for iPhone. Type in the parts you need, and it works out how to cut them from your sheets and boards with the least waste — accounting for the width your saw blade takes out.

Every layout is checked before you see it. When a plan is provably the best possible one, the app says so; when it isn't, it tells you how far off it is instead of pretending. One price, every feature, no subscription, and it works with no signal in a basement shop.
