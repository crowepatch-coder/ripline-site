Privacy Policy — Ripline

Last updated: 17 September 2026

The short version

Ripline collects nothing. There is no account, no email signup, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your projects, parts lists, stock, offcuts, and settings are stored on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere.

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings (it is off by default), that data syncs through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It goes to Apple, under your Apple Account, not to us. We have no access to it and no servers of our own.

Network use

The app makes one kind of network request, and only if you ask for it:

  1. Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) — only if you turn on iCloud sync, which is off by default.

There are no in-app purchases, so the app contains no payment code at all. You bought it from the App Store; Apple handled that, and we never see your payment details. The app is fully usable with no network connection.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Since the app collects no data, any change would be a clarification rather than a new data practice.

Contact

Questions: riplinesupport@gmail.com
