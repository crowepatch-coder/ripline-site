Getting started

Open the Bookshelf (Sample) project and tap Layout. That is a real optimized cut plan — you can explore it before entering anything of your own.

To start your own: Projects → +, add part lines (name, length, width, quantity), then check the Layout tab. Results update as you type.

Common questions

Why doesn't a part fit? The Layout tab lists every unplaced part with the specific reason — too large, grain-locked so it can't be rotated, no matching material, or your stock quantity ran out. Each reason suggests what to change.

What does "Proven optimal" mean? The app computes a mathematical lower bound on how many sheets or boards any possible layout would need. When your plan matches that bound, no better plan exists and the badge says so. When it doesn't, the app tells you how many pieces away from that bound you are rather than claiming perfection.

My cuts came out slightly wrong. Check your kerf setting against your actual blade — measure a test cut, don't trust the preset. Also verify your sheet's real thickness: "3/4 inch" plywood is usually about 23/32".

Why is my sheet's usable size smaller than the sheet? If you set edge trim, the app removes that material first, as factory edges are rarely straight or square.

Can I use millimeters? Yes. Set the project's units in the project options menu, or type units explicitly at any time — 597mm, 23-1/2, 1' 11-1/2", and 59.7cm all work regardless of the project setting.
